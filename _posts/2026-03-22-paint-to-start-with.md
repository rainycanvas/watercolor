The variety of watercolor paints is staggering, with some brands offering over 200 colors. However, you don't need a massive collection to start. While it’s possible to paint with just 3 primary colors, I believe 8 to 12 colors is the "sweet spot" - it provides a perfect balance between mixing convenience and avoiding an overwhelming palette.

Throughout this post, I might nerd out and talk your ear off about the technical side of things. If you find the theory a bit too dry, don’t worry - I’ve placed all the specific pigment recommendations in a clear table below. Feel free to jump directly to those if you just want to get your palette ready.

<h2>Primary Triads</h2>

The minimum starting point are the primaries. 

|  Name  | Pigment Code |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Yellow** | PY97 or PY3 |
| **Magenta** | PV19 or PR122 |
| **Cyan** | PB15:3 or PB16 |
